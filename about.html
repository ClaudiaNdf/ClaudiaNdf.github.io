About me

I am a researcher at the University of Potsdam working in empirical educational research and educational psychology.

Academic background

I completed my PhD in Educational Science at the University of Potsdam (2022), following an MSc and BSc in Psychology at Friedrich Schiller University Jena. My dissertation examined high-achieving students in Germany, focusing on definitions, development and social integration.

Professional experience

Before joining the University of Potsdam in 2023, I worked at the University of Tübingen and at the Research Data Centre of the Institute for Educational Quality Improvement at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin.
