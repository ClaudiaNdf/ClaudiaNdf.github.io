Research

Research interests

My work draws on educational psychology and empirical educational research to understand the experiences and development of students in schools.

Classroom heterogeneity

Classroom composition, multidimensional heterogeneity and their relationships to psychosocial student outcomes.

Social relationships

Peer networks, social integration and classroom-level social structures.

High-achieving students

How high achievement is defined, how competencies develop, and how high-achieving students are socially integrated.

Open science and methodology

Reproducible research, multiverse analysis, research data infrastructures and data sharing.
