Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of educational psychology, social psychology, sociology and education. I am particularly interested in students’ peer relationships in the classroom and how they shape learning and development.

A central question in my work is how multidimensional classroom composition influences relationship patterns and how we can create classroom environments beneficial for children and adolescents. I enjoy interdisciplinary collaboration and believe that considering multiple perspectives helps advance science.
